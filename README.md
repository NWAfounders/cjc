# CJC Development workspace

Tools and working notes for [CJC Development](https://cjc-development.com), Cameron Clark's
Fayetteville, Arkansas acquisitions and development firm.

> This repository is public. Keep investor, deal-term, lender and contractor details out of it
> until it is made private.

## The company (public record)

CJC Development does community value-add commercial real estate across Northwest Arkansas:
acquisitions, development and asset management.

**Cameron Clark**, founder. Fayetteville native; University of Arkansas 2015. Director of business
development at B-Unlimited, then from June 2020 an executive broker at Bennett Commercial Real Estate
(retail and office): $105M+ in transactions, 631,700+ sq ft sold or leased and 116+ acres of land over
about 4.5 years. NWA Business Journal Fast 15 (2023). Co-host of the NWA Founders podcast.

## Projects (as reported in the press)

| Project | Summary |
|---|---|
| **Wilson Parkview Condos**, 15 W. Prospect St., Fayetteville | Three 1948 brick apartment buildings on Wilson Park restored as 20 condos; on the National Register of Historic Places since Feb 2026; for sale from Aug 2026 at $300k to $400k through NWA Home & Spaces. |
| **South School Strip**, S. School Ave., Fayetteville | 2.1-acre retail redevelopment (former Dollar General, pawn shop and Rick's Iron Skillet buildings) with Lefler Capital and Burke Larkin. Tenants: Fossil Cove Brewing (second location) and Next Gear Outdoors. Completion expected Q1 2027. |
| **2400 Township**, 2044 E. Township St., Fayetteville | Former Township Baptist Church south of Gulley Park: the 7,450 sf church becomes a restaurant, plus 8 townhomes. Rezoned to NS-G. Architect: Matt Poe, STOKE Architecture; civil: Gavin Smith Civil Engineering. |
| **Block Avenue**, downtown Fayetteville | 102 N. Block Ave. (former Tables & Ale), 5,438 sf, bought 2022 by Block Street Corner LLC. Home to a B-Unlimited store (opened Apr 2024) and a restaurant space (Tula). The CJC site also lists 110-116 Block Street. |
